# Slide Layout Archetypes

Presentation designs fall into structured archetypes based on their narrative function.

---

## 1. Context & Problem Framing (1/3 + 2/3 Split)
- **Left Column (1/3)**: High-level takeaway callout, large bold statement, or contextual image/statistic.
- **Right Column (2/3)**: 2–3 structured cards detailing drivers, regulatory landscape, or industry dynamics.

## 2. Research Gap & Bridge (3-Stage Flow)
- **Stage 1 (Left Card)**: Established Literature & Prior Findings ("Evidence is mixed on ESG disclosure...").
- **Stage 2 (Middle Card / Bridge)**: The Unexplored Context / Conflict ("Limited evidence in emerging markets & POJK 51 enforcement...").
- **Stage 3 (Right Card / Focus)**: Core Research Question & Contribution ("Does ESG Disclosure improve Financial Reporting Quality?").

## 3. Empirical Model & Methodology Specification
- **Top Block**: Mathematical / econometric equation displayed in a highlighted accent box.
- **Bottom Grid**: 3-column definition cards:
  - Dependent Variable ($FRQ_{it}$) & Measurement formula
  - Independent Variable ($ESGPerf_{it}$) & Scoring pillars
  - Control Variables & Fixed Effects ($Size, Lev, ROA, Year, Industry$)

## 4. Empirical Results & Regression Tables
- **Left/Center (65% width)**: Structured statistical table with clean alignment, clear standard errors, and significance stars (`*`, `**`, `***`).
- **Right Panel (35% width)**: 
  - Callout Card 1: Key Finding & Economic Magnitude
  - Callout Card 2: Hypothesis Confirmation / Theoretical Link

## 5. Robustness & Heterogeneity Checks
- **Side-by-Side Comparison**: 2 columns comparing Subsample A (e.g., Manufacturing) vs Subsample B (Non-Manufacturing) or Alternative Proxies (Modified Jones vs Kothari).
- **Summary Banner (Bottom)**: Single-line takeaway validating stability across alternative specifications.

## 6. Strategic Implications & Synthesis
- **3 Vertical Pillars**:
  1. *Theoretical Contributions* (Trade-off Theory, Stakeholder Agency)
  2. *Managerial & Corporate Practice* (ESG integration, internal reporting controls)
  3. *Policy & Regulatory Governance* (OJK disclosure mandates, enforcement mechanisms)
