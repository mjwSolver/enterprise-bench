# Slide Prompt Patterns & Templates

Templates for generating image and visual asset prompts for various slide categories.

---

## Pattern 1: Conceptual Trade-Off / Theory Slide

```text
Create one 16:9 full-slide PowerPoint image for an academic seminar presentation.

Slide role: theoretical framework / Trade-Off Theory.

Visible text:
- Header: THEORETICAL FOUNDATION | 05
- Main title: Trade-Off Theory in ESG Strategy
- Left Card: Costs & Resource Drain (Agency costs, compliance friction, short-term earnings pressure)
- Center Node: Managerial Balancing Act
- Right Card: Value Creation & Signaling (Reputation capital, lower cost of debt, enhanced reporting quality)
- Bottom takeaway: Net reporting quality depends on governance discipline and transparency mechanisms.

Design: modern consulting report, Swiss design grid, crisp pale off-white background (#F8F9FA), deep teal (#0F766E) and slate navy (#1E293B) typography, warm amber accent (#D97706), thin hairline dividers, generous whitespace, clear balanced visual hierarchy. No cartoon icons, no stock photos of people, no 3D globes, no dark gradients.
```

## Pattern 2: Process Funnel & Research Sampling

```text
Create one 16:9 full-slide PowerPoint graphic for an empirical methodology slide.

Slide role: sample selection funnel and filtering criteria.

Visible text:
- Header: METHODOLOGY | SAMPLE SELECTION | 06
- Main title: Sample Filtering and Selection Criteria
- Step 1: Initial population of listed Indonesian firms (2017–2023)
- Step 2: Excluding financial and banking institutions due to distinct accounting frameworks
- Step 3: Excluding firms with missing Bloomberg ESG disclosure scores
- Step 4 (Final Highlighted Box): Final balanced panel of 630 firm-year observations

Design: modern executive presentation, linear vertical or horizontal funnel with clean rectangular step stages, pure white card containers over soft cool-gray background, deep teal borders, amber badge for final sample count, large readable typography, zero clutter.
```
